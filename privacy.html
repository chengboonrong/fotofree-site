Privacy Policy

FotoFree · Effective 20 November 2026

FotoFree doesn’t collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Your photos

FotoFree asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and apply the choices you make. Your photos never leave your device. The app does not download photos stored only in iCloud. Deletions and favorites are made through Apple’s Photos framework, and deletions always ask for your confirmation in Apple’s own prompt.

To describe a photo’s subject, FotoFree uses Apple’s Vision framework on the device. Those labels, along with which photos you have already reviewed, are stored only on the device, in the app’s own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

What FotoFree does not do

  • It makes no network requests of its own and has no servers.
  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party code.

Purchases

The optional Unlimited Batches purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. FotoFree checks the purchase on your device and never sees your payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

FotoFree collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
